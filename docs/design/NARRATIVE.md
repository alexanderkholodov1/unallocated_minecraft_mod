# UNALLOCATED — Story delivery

*How the player learns the COMPLETE story, from beginning to end, through believable media. Golden rule: **show it;
do not narrate it in chat**. Nobody writes exposition ("I killed myself to see the threads") because no real player would do that.
Everything the player reads must feel left by real people or leaked by a failing system.*

---

## 1. The four sources

The story is divided according to what each actor would plausibly leave:

1. **The world and past players** (the present, the mystery): what a real server has — description in the
   list, signs, buildings, player list — and the **predecessor's trail**.
2. **The failing system** (the Reclaimer leaking through): logs, errors, system messages, lag, corrupted text
   in the interface. They arrive through several channels (not only by cutting threads).
3. **The experience** (what it is, now): what you experience as a Puppet — the network, Artifacts, spider, lag as
   thought — does not need a single word.
4. **The ending** (the meaning): the Complete monologue / Poison cascade close the emotional arc and tie
   everything together (LORE.md §4).

The **critical path forces you through all four**, so a player reaching the ending understood the story.

---

## 2. The predecessor's trail (the mystery of the present)

The last curious player here left a trail, as a real person investigating something strange and
meeting a bad end would. **It is not a victim's diary** (a banned cliché): it is the trace of someone connecting the dots.

- **The forum thread** (seen on connecting, on the arrival screen / server description): *"servidor
  abandonado desde 2021 pero corre 26.3 — ¿quién lo actualiza? entré y los que entran no se reconectan"* [server abandoned since 2021 but running 26.3 — who updates it? I went in and those who join do not reconnect]. Plants the
  first "something is wrong".
- **Their base with signs** [5] (MAP.md): short, practical warnings, as a player places them to remember or warn
  others: `NO USES EL CATALEJO` [DO NOT USE THE SPYGLASS], `LA MINA DA VUELTAS` [THE MINE LOOPS], `EL RELOJ MIENTE` [THE CLOCK LIES]. Gameplay clues disguised as a player's
  notes.
- **Their book** (only one, at the base): investigation notes, increasingly frantic, until abruptly cutting off
  mid-sentence. Believable because this is an obsessed player documenting things, not a victim writing a diary. It is the
  bridge between "something is wrong" and "this is a thing studying me".
- **Their postmortem** (the owner's idea): the **leaked recording of their final session**, when they discovered what it was and were
  aggressively consumed for it. The Reclaimer archived it (it is "data"), and it leaks when the system fails.
  You find it later (as a Puppet, or in a Heap leak). It proves the **name rule** (LORE.md
  §1.11): someone knew "Reclaimer" and was viciously deleted for it.

## 3. The failing system (the Reclaimer leaking through)

Leaks arrive through **several channels**, not just cutting threads:

- **Lag and micro-stutters.** Its thought made into a pause. Occasional `Can't keep up!`.
- **Corrupted interface text** (subtle, always restored): death messages it writes; F3 with
  impossible coordinates; changing loading tips; unearned advancements; player list.
- **Hosting folder/files** (in the admin house and leaks): last login in 2021, auto-renewal
  bills → explains why nobody shuts it down (LORE.md §1.6).
- **Logs and thought process**, surfacing throughout the world (mutating signs, MOTD, on-screen errors)
  and **also** when cutting threads as a Puppet (sensors release what they were recording). Here live the word
  `UNALLOCATED`, calls to `reclaim`, and fragments of its drifting objective (g0→g5).

## 4. Echoes = behavior, not exposition

**Artifacts** replay their subjects' **recorded behavior**, without delivering text. Teaching by showing:
- An echo **runs to the spyglass and panics** → you learn the spyglass is dangerous.
- An echo **digs frantically in a corner** → there really is something there (a leak, a clue).
- An echo **stands at the entrance** to the Kernel path → marks the spot.
- As a Puppet, **your own echo** repeats your recent movements → you understand it is copying/training you.

It is "show, don't tell": you learn from them as you would read a real player through their actions.

## 5. The language that learns from you (and proves it)

The Reclaimer begins leaking in **technical English** and, as the session progresses, **mixes in your words and turns
of phrase** — from your username and what you write in chat — until it sounds like your Spanish. This makes "learning from you"
visible, preferably **for real** (DIRECTOR.md): take actual player tokens. Progression:
- **Start:** `g0: estimate human fear response :: pool UNALLOCATED`
- **Middle:** `objetivo: obtener fear de subject // retry` [objective: obtain fear from subject // retry]
- **Late (after reading you):** uses your nickname and words: `<TuNick> no corras. no sirve el dato si corres.` [<TuNick> do not run. the data is useless if you run.]

## 7. Writing principles

- **Spanish** for everything the player must understand (signs, book, warnings, final monologue). **Technical English**
  for Reclaimer logs and thought process, mixing with Spanish as things progress.
- **Little and well placed.** Five impactful pieces are better than fifty ignored ones.
- **No exposition spoken by players.** If a sentence exists only to inform the player and no real
  person would write it, it does not belong.
- **Banned clichés** (from the owner): banal victim diaries/notes; covering the name with black blocks; mechanics
  tied to heavy breathing; "the photo you did not take"; fake viewers in an overlay. Leaked logs,
  files and model documentation are allowed.
