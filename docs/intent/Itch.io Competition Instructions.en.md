First-person horror
One week to turn Minecraft into a nightmare.

Create a horror mod designed to be played in first person. Build an experience that generates tension, surprises those who play it and turns the familiar into something unsettling.

Organized by the Computer Society of USFQ and COCOA USFQ. Participation is exclusive to students of Universidad San Francisco de Quito.

Dates
Registration: October 9 to 20, 2026.
Development: October 21 to 28, 2026.
Awards: November 6, 2026.
Opening and closing times will be announced in continental Ecuador time (UTC−5).

Who can participate?
You can participate individually or in a team of at most three members. All members must be USFQ students.

The challenge
Develop a mod for Minecraft Java 26.3, using Forge or Fabric, that offers a first-person horror experience.

You can explore psychological horror, suspense, creatures, exploration, survival or any other idea that fits the theme. You decide how to provoke fear.

We recommend an experience of 5 to 15 minutes, with a clear beginning and enough content for the jury to get to know your proposal. You can include a map if your mod needs it (it is not required).

Rules
The content to be judged must be developed during the jam, October 21 to 28.
Each team may have at most three members.
Mods for Forge or Fabric are accepted. It is not necessary to offer compatibility with both.
The experience must be designed to be played in first person.
You may use external libraries and resources with permission of use, as long as you declare and credit them.
A modpack alone does not count as a submission: the project must include an original mod developed by the team.
Include warnings if your experience contains flashing lights, loud sounds or other sensitive content.
Participants keep authorship of their projects. By participating, they authorize the organizers to show images and gameplay of the project in the jam's promotion and awards.
What must you submit?
Publish your project on itch.io and submit it to this jam before the deadline. The project page must include:

Mod file and list of dependencies.
Loader and exact version needed to run it.
Installation instructions and how to start playing.
Map and configuration, if needed.
Brief description of the experience.
Team members and credits for external resources.
Known bugs and content warnings.
We recommend including screenshots and a short gameplay video to present your project.

Judging
All projects will compete in a single category: best first-person horror experience.

Criterion

What we evaluate

Fear factor

Did it make us feel fear, get nervous or jump out of our seats? Both sustained tension and well-executed scares are valued, as is the overall atmosphere of the experience.

Creativity

Originality of the idea and use of Minecraft to create horror.

Mechanics and first-person experience

How the interaction and mechanics contribute to the experience.

Stability and ease of installation

That the mod works and has clear instructions.

Sound and visual presentation

How the audio and the visual elements reinforce the horror.

Prizes
There will be prizes! We will announce the details and the distribution of prizes before the November 6 awards.

Get ready to enter
Form your team, prepare your tools and bring an idea that makes someone hesitate before opening Minecraft again.

See you on October 21.
