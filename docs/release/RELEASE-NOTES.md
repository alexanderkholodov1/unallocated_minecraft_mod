UNALLOCATED 0.4.0 — a first-person horror mod for **Minecraft Java Edition 26.3** (Fabric), by NULL_Threshold Interactive.

## Install
- **Minecraft 26.3** with **Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or newer**, Java 25 (the launcher provides it).
- Put **only `unallocated-0.4.0.jar`** in your `mods` folder. Fabric API is already inside the JAR.
- **TLauncher / offline accounts work.** For a TLauncher custom version the folder is `.minecraft/versions/<version name>/mods`.
- Start the game, accept the contract (type **I agree** / **De acuerdo**, or click), then press **UNALLOCATED** above Singleplayer.
- Stuck? Open the pause menu and press **I'm stuck**, or type `/unallocated next` (`/unallocated siguiente`).
- Fallback: unzip `UNALLOCATED-world.zip` into `saves/` and open it from Singleplayer.

## What's new since 0.3.0 (rebuilt after the owner's own playtest)
- **An intro before the world**: who you are and why you are joining, drawn at random per copy from three arrivals
  (an explorer who read a forum post, a former player coming back, a friend's strange invitation), then the server as
  it appears in a multiplayer list — 0/20 players, yet names when you hover.
- **A believable public server**: ValleCraft Network on real terrain, with a network hub (dead Minigames/Plots/Creative
  portals, rules, a leaderboard, parkour), a staff house, claimed player builds and a ruined arena; a title on join,
  server-style chat, a working Tab list where names appear and leave, a sidebar scoreboard and hub items (server
  selector, rules book, welcome-kit spyglass).
- **Death and the Puppet state**: the death screen lets you stay dead ("Not yet") as a Puppet who sees the threads; press
  R to consent and come back to life whenever you want.
- **Threads are 3D**, our own models crossing the world where its scripts run, visible only to a Puppet; shears cut them.
- **The monster** appears only at the first death and at the climax; it can no longer get stuck behind walls.
- **No softlocks**: no blocks that slow you or stop your jumps, every room has an exit, every chapter nudges a lost
  player and moves on by itself, and the pause menu always offers a rescue.
- The Heap is rotten all at once (never block by block in front of you); the ambient bed no longer beeps.
- Fully bilingual (English / Spanish, follows your game language), including signs, books, the Tab list and the scoreboard.

Content warnings: flashing and flickering, loud sudden sounds, jump scares, psychological horror.
